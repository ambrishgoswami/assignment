Frequently Asked Questions

Is studying in Germany really tuition-free?

Most public universities charge no tuition for undergraduate and many master's programmes; a small semester contribution applies.

Do I need to know German?

Many programmes are English-taught. German helps for daily life and part-time work, and we offer A1–B2 coaching.

What is a blocked account?

A deposit account proving you can fund your living costs, required for the German student visa.

Is the counselling really free?

Yes — profile evaluation and first counselling session are completely free.
